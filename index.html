♞Pawn Coach Play, and a coach watches every move: it names the opening you're in, catches the mistake, and shows you how to think — not just the engine's move. Built to take you from beginner toward mastery. 100% offline — no downloads, no accounts, no API.
Pawn Coach runs entirely in your browser — the chess engine, the analysis, and your saved games never leave this page. It's one HTML file with no dependencies, so it's trivially open-source: save it, read it, put it on GitHub, open it offline. The coach is tuned for beginners: it reliably catches hung pieces, free material, and one-move mates — the mistakes that decide games under 800. It is not a 3000-Elo engine and won't find deep 5-move tactics, by design. Import your games from chess.com via Games → your game → ⋯ → Download (or Archive → Download) to get the PGN, then paste it here.
